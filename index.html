How It Works

Three steps between you
and zero wasted food.

No complicated setup. No lengthy onboarding. Just open the app and start saving.

Snap a photo, give it a name, set an expiration date, and pick where it lives. Takes about 10 seconds. Seriously.

JUST. nudges you a couple of days before something expires, so dinner practically plans itself — sauté the spinach tonight, roast the carrots tomorrow.
